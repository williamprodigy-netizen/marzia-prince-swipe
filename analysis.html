Analysis

What they do, why it works, and what is worth taking.

The one-line read. This is not a solo creator competing with us. It is an agency-operated funnel with a creator front, aimed one rung up the ladder from our customer — at the creator who already earns $1K–$5K a month and cannot break $10K. They sell the ceiling. We sell the start.

Worth taking

Sell the VIP pass before the event

$37 on a free masterclass, with the slide deck as the headline bonus — delivered only when the session ends. It monetises registration and buys stay-rate at zero marginal cost. We currently monetise nothing between registration and the room.

Put the disqualifier in the copy

A written “NOT for you if” list beside the “FOR you if” list, on the registration page. It filters get-rich-quick traffic before the form rather than after the setter has spent an hour on it.

Bookend with a scored question

Same 1–10 confidence question at the open and the close. The prospect measures their own movement and types it publicly. Cheap, and it manufactures the “something changed” feeling the close depends on.

Name the suspicion first

“You've probably attended masterclasses before… at the end they hit you with a pitch.” Saying it before the audience thinks it converts a defence into rapport.

A group chat, not a broadcast list

WhatsApp group as Step 1 on the confirmation page. Registrants see each other's momentum, which a one-to-one SMS thread cannot manufacture. Worth testing against AI-LNS rather than replacing it.

Insecurity as the qualification

She converts the exact reason a woman believes she cannot do this — her age, her skin, her weight — into the reason brands will pay her. That is a stronger emotional move than any income claim on the page.

Where they are weak

The calendar link is broken

Add-to-Calendar writes 22 July 2026 on a funnel selling 4 August. Three pages still reference July 21st. Anyone diligent enough to save the event is the person most likely to attend, and they are being sent to a dead date.

The replay is a free download

Two hours of their best sales asset, ungated, resolving to a direct MP4. It cost nothing to take.

The funnel is bought, not built

Leftover alt text from another operator's funnel, duplicate slugs, stale dates. Whatever House of Leap is good at, maintaining this one is not it.

No guarantee at all

She turns it into a virtue — “I don't want to sell on guarantees and fancy promises” — but it is still a missing risk reversal against an unstated price.
