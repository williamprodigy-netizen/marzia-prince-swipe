What matters

Marzia is the face, not the operator

The Zoom confirmation replies to christian@houseofleap.com. House of Leap is a four-partner Danish growth agency that builds and runs funnels for creator-founders — Marzia is listed on their site alongside Loop Fitness and three fitness coaches. This is structurally the same business Will runs behind Gloria. The competitor is not a solo creator who got lucky; it is an agency with a roster.

They sell the ceiling, we sell the start

Their “FOR you if” list names the customer precisely: “You're already making $1,000 to $5,000 a month, but you're feeling stuck.” That is a sharper, more solvent segment than the beginner. A creator already earning $3K has proven they can do the work and has cash flow to pay from.

The price is never stated &mdash; on purpose

Eleven captured pages, three emails and 26,074 transcribed words contain no number. An attendee named Michelle asks live, “do you have to have the call tonight to lock in the price and bonuses?” and Marzia answers about honouring bonuses without ever naming a figure. Instead she anchors: “This is not a cheap $47 ebook. It's not just a $500 course.” and states plainly “I'm NOT going to drop a checkout link.”

The slide deck is the show-up-and-stay bribe

The VIP stack promises “every slide from the live masterclass delivered straight to your inbox when we're done. No scrambling to screenshot mid-session.” It costs nothing to deliver, it can only be earned by sitting through the pitch, and it is sold for $37 before the event.

WhatsApp, not email, is the pre-class channel

Step 1 on both thank-you pages is joining a WhatsApp group, pitched as content “not available anywhere else”. They pull registrants off email into a group chat they control. UGC World does this job over SMS (AI-LNS) — a group is a different animal, because registrants see each other's enthusiasm.

She pre-empts the pitch objection, twice

A slide reads: “at the end they hit you with a pitch and you realize the whole thing was just a sales presentation disguised as a class.” She names the audience's suspicion out loud, early, then again later — then pitches anyway. Disarming the objection before it forms.

A commitment loop bookends the class

She asks “on a scale of 1 to 10, how confident are you that you know how to earn consistent $10K months?” and makes them type a number in chat. At the close she asks the same question again. The delta is the sale.

Ungated two-hour replay

The full masterclass sits on /replay behind no login, no token and no email gate. The Wistia media resolves to a direct MP4 — that is how this capture was made with plain curl. Scarcity copy does the work an expiry usually does.

The funnel is unmaintained, and it costs them show rate

The registration page sells Tue 4 Aug. Both thank-you pages say “in the lead up to July 21st”. The pre-frame page says “Tuesday, July 21st”. And the Add-to-Calendar link hardcodes dates=20260722T000000Z. Every registrant who saves it to their calendar books a date that has already passed. The founder photo's alt text still reads “Leo Grundström” from whichever funnel this template came from.

Two slugs, one page

/breakdown and /what-is-UGC serve byte-identical content — a 22,760px-tall teaching page. Same asset, two entry points, presumably for two different traffic sources.

Their proof set is not all-female

27 Wistia testimonials on the call-confirmation page; of the 12 named on-page, three are men. Their age angle is explicit too: “There are almost none over 40 or 50, and the brands selling to that customer are desperate for someone who looks like them.” Ours is a deliberately narrower set. Worth deciding, not drifting into.

The insecurity reframe is the emotional core

She runs slides listing HAIRLOSS / ACNE / WEIGHT GAIN / WRINKLES over her own photos, then: “Flaws and insecurities that you think are working against you, are the things that make you authentic. Authenticity is what sells.” She converts the reason a woman thinks she can't do this into the reason she can.

Night two is buyers-only

“Tomorrow, I go into the deep dive of how to prepare for Q4… and that call is only available in the fast track.” A second session gated behind purchase, announced live, on the biggest-seasonality argument of the year.

No ad-library coverage

GAP Gethookd has no brand record for UGC Fasttrack, so creative volume, spend and survival rate are unmeasured. The Meta pixel (1135267478240733), Clarity (xufintorxl) and Contentsquare are all confirmed on the live pages, but nothing here supports a claim about their traffic.
